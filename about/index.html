Our community · Established 2006

Home is
a feeling.

A familiar language. A shared meal. People who make a new city feel a little more like your own.

That’s what the Hong Kong Student Network is here for. Founded in 2006 under the Students’ Society of McGill University, we celebrate Hong Kong’s rich culture and create a home away from home.

Through Cantonese mini-classes, dim sum outings, and mahjong competitions, we make room for friendship, connection, and a sense of belonging.
