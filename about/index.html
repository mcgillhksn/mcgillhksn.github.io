About HKSN

Founded in 2006 under the Students’ Society of McGill University, the Hong Kong Student Network celebrates Hong Kong’s rich culture and creates a home away from home.

Through Cantonese mini-classes, dim sum outings, and mahjong competitions, we make room for friendship, connection, and a sense of belonging.
